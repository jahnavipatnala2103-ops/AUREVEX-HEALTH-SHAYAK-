# HealthSahayak — Product Outcomes

Native project Todo is not exposed in this session, so this file preserves the full outcome criteria before implementation. Items remain open until their behavior is implemented.

4. A few secondary Profile/access/settings labels remain English even though the primary Home, Copilot, Vault, document-review, Journey and data-flow flows support English, Telugu and Hindi.
- [ ] **Responsive HealthSahayak shell and Home** — Deliver a clean, minimal, premium, calm, trustworthy, accessible, professional and responsive application for desktop, laptop, tablet and mobile. Use a light/clean background, one consistent healthcare accent color, clear typography, strong hierarchy, clear icons, good spacing and professional rounded components. Avoid excessive gradients/animation, neon, futuristic AI graphics, large text blocks, too many floating cards, unnecessary charts, complicated navigation, generic SaaS styling and obvious AI-generated patterns. Keep complexity in intelligence/data flow, not on screen; keep mobile layouts simple and navigation natural. Main navigation is exactly Home, Copilot, Health Vault, Journey and Profile; Medications, Appointments, Insurance, Family, Caregiver, Privacy, Settings and Emergency belong within relevant sections, especially Profile and Home. Home answers “What needs my attention right now?” and displays “Good morning, [User Name]”, profile picture, notifications, profile/settings access, “How can I help with your health today?”, Ask Copilot and Voice, and suggested questions “What happened in my last appointment?”, “Explain my latest report.”, “What medications are currently recorded?”, and “What do I need to do next?” Include a small Today area for upcoming appointment, medication reminder, pending follow-up and recently added health record; include a compact Healthcare Journey preview and View Full Journey (example: Today follow-up pending; Oct 6 doctor consultation; Oct 5 blood report added). Do not overcrowd the dashboard. Communicate “Your AI-powered personal healthcare journey companion,” “We don't just store health information. We turn it into a connected healthcare journey,” and “From answers to context.”

- [x] **Medical document upload, intelligence and human review** — Health Vault visibly offers Scan with Camera and Upload Document and accepts camera/photo, PDF, JPG and PNG for prescriptions, lab/diagnostic reports, doctor notes, discharge summaries, medical records, insurance documents and applicable wellness information. Flow is Upload/Scan → Processing → Document Detection → OCR/Extraction → AI Found Information → User Review → Confirm & Save. Identify document type. Extract only source-present information: prescription medicine, dosage, frequency/instructions, duration if present, date and doctor if available; lab test, result, unit, provided reference range, date and whether outside the reference range printed on that report; doctor-note date, explicitly stated symptoms, assessment/diagnosis and recommendations; discharge admission/discharge dates, stated diagnoses/procedures, medications and follow-up instructions; insurance provider, policy information, dates and document metadata. Never invent missing information. Before sending an uploaded document for AI processing, disclose the sample/AI-processing boundary and require user confirmation. Show “AI found the following information” and clear extracted fields. Let the user edit; uncertain extraction is never saved automatically. Explicit Confirm & Save persists the reviewed document/structured data, updates Health Profile where appropriate, creates a Timeline event and makes relevant information available to Copilot without requiring duplicate manual entry. Unconfirmed information does not update the profile/timeline/Copilot context. Provide processing/error/retry states without pretending a failure succeeded.

- [x] **Plain-language report summaries and Health Vault** — Every important medical record has a plain-language summary with “IMPORTANT FINDINGS”, “WHAT THIS MEANS” and “SOURCE”. Clearly say when a result is outside the reference range shown on the report; a value outside range alone does not establish a diagnosis and must be interpreted with symptoms and other clinical information. Explain simply, do not diagnose from a single value, claim medical certainty, or tell a user to start prescription medication; recommend appropriate professional follow-up when relevant. Health Vault title is “YOUR HEALTH VAULT” and subtitle is “Your important health information, organized in one place.” Include categories All, Reports, Prescriptions, Doctor Notes, Discharge and Insurance, search, filter and sort Newest → Oldest / Oldest → Newest. Show clean document cards (e.g. Blood Test Report — Lab Report · Oct 8, 2026; Doctor Prescription · Oct 6, 2026; Discharge Summary · Sep 28, 2026); do not make a complicated file manager.

- [x] **Unified Health Profile and supporting care organizers** — Profile brings together personal information, health information, medications, appointments, health records, timeline, emergency information, insurance, family/caregiver access and privacy settings. Basic fields: name, profile picture, date of birth, age, gender, blood group and preferred language; health fields: allergies, existing conditions and important health information; emergency contact and emergency information. Users can edit their information. Keep Personal Information, Health Information, Emergency, Insurance, Family & Care, Privacy & Access and Account & Security within Profile. Medication management is an organizer, not a prescription system: show medication name, instructions as recorded, start date, prescribing doctor if available, source prescription and “VIEW SOURCE PRESCRIPTION”; never prescribe new medication, change dosage or generate a treatment plan. Appointments stay simple and connect to Journey: upcoming/previous doctor, date, time and status, filters Upcoming/Completed/Cancelled and sort Newest/Oldest. Tasks/next steps include items such as complete blood test, follow up with doctor, review prescription and upload discharge summary; users can mark tasks complete. AI suggestions remain user-controlled and no important healthcare action happens automatically. Inside Profile, Insurance covers provider, policy information, relevant dates and insurance documents.

- [x] **User-controlled family, caregiver, doctor and privacy access** — Support patient/individual, family member and caregiver access without building separate applications. Patients control what others see. Represent the requested example permissions (Family Member A: Appointments ✓, Medications ✓, Reports ✕; Caregiver B: Appointments ✓, Tasks ✓, Reports ✕), provide Manage Access and Revoke Access, and make visible who has access, what they can access and permission status in Profile → Privacy & Access. Privacy title is “Your information is yours.” A simple doctor role sees only expressly authorized patient information: Patient, Relevant Records, Healthcare Timeline, Medications and Appointments, never unrelated private information. Access is user-controlled and category-scoped. Do not claim enterprise-level security unless implemented; do not claim invitations, access or deletion behavior that does not actually work.

- [x] **Healthcare Journey and user-controlled next steps** — Create a clean chronological timeline that automatically updates after a confirmed document or healthcare event, represents the healthcare journey rather than a list of files, and links its entries to source records. Example: Oct 8 Blood Test Report — 2 findings detected; Oct 6 Doctor Consultation — prescription added; Oct 4 Blood Test; Sep 28 Hospital Discharge. Include filters All, Consultations, Reports, Medications, Appointments, Follow-ups and sorting Newest/Oldest. Appointment records connect to the timeline. Include next-step tasks the user can mark complete; AI-generated suggestions stay under user control.

- [x] **Safe, context-aware Copilot with evidence and history** — Copilot is not a generic chatbot. It answers using relevant authorized records from recent reports/lab results, prescriptions/current medications, consultations, allergies, existing conditions, appointments and timeline, and does not send all personal health information into every answer. Support questions “What does my latest report mean?”, “What did my doctor say?”, “What medication was prescribed?”, “What happened in my last appointment?”, “What should I do next?” and symptoms such as “I am having fever.” For a symptom, check relevant authorized context first and respond cautiously, without immediately prescribing. Distinguish “WHAT WE KNOW”, “WHAT IS GENERAL INFORMATION” and “WHAT IS UNCERTAIN”. Never prescribe, recommend starting prescription medication, change dosage, diagnose with certainty, pretend to be a healthcare professional or claim certainty when information is incomplete. If information is insufficient, say: “I don't have enough information in your health records to determine this. Please check the original document or speak with your healthcare professional.” Explain records/existing medications, connect events, help track follow-ups and suggest speaking with a healthcare professional when appropriate. Important contextual answers have an expandable “WHY AM I SEEING THIS?” showing exact sources/dates (e.g. Blood Test Report — Oct 8; Doctor Consultation — Oct 6; Prescription — Oct 6). Save previous conversations (“My blood report”, “Last appointment”, “What do I need to do?”, “My medications”); clicking one reopens it.

- [ ] **English, Telugu and Hindi interface summaries and voice** — Prioritize English, Telugu and Hindi. Copilot responses, health summaries and important interface text can display in all three languages. Support voice input and voice output in English, Telugu and Hindi; convert speech to text, respond, and optionally speak the response. Show clear LISTENING, PROCESSING and SPEAKING states. Do not claim bilingual handwritten OCR. Provide a clear fallback when browser/device voice capability is unavailable.

- [ ] **Verified application sessions and role-aware data** — Establish a real application session only after verified email OTP or explicitly chosen Manus OAuth fallback; keep it HTTP-only, secure in HTTPS Preview, and bound to the starter's app-session cookie contract. Resolve the authenticated user from the verified server session, use owner-scoped health data and never accept a client-supplied user ID. Support Patient/Individual, Family Member, Caregiver and Doctor roles without separate apps; retain the trusted project-owner admin and prohibit self-assigned Admin. A real account never uses the public demo password, and the demo never creates an authenticated user. Support session logout, profile creation/editing and provider-managed account recovery without inventing a local password flow.

- [x] **ABDM/ABHA readiness, prototype honesty and explainable architecture** — Structure simplified records conceptually around Patient, Observation, Medication/MedicationRequest, Encounter, DiagnosticReport, DocumentReference and Condition. If an ABHA ID is displayed, label it exactly “DEMO / MOCK ABHA ID”; do not claim real ABDM integration. Demo data is realistic but clearly synthetic. Never fabricate doctors, hospitals, partnerships, clinical accuracy, outcomes, production security, diagnoses or medical recommendations. Clearly distinguish synthetic sample/demo data from saved personal health information; do not display “Hackathon prototype” as customer-facing branding. Keep the explainable flow User → Upload/Camera → OCR/Document Processing → Document-Type Detection → Information Extraction → Validation/User Confirmation → Structured Health Data → Health Profile + Health Vault → Healthcare Timeline → Relevant Context Retrieval → AI Copilot → Safety Check → Explanation/Next Step → User Confirmation → Tracking. The central differentiator is connected context, not more pages or secondary admin workflows.

- [x] **Complete hackathon walkthrough and maintainable implementation** — The demonstration can open HealthSahayak; upload a synthetic blood/lab report; show processing, extraction of test names/values/units/dates/reference ranges and simple-language summary; let the user review and confirm; show the document in Vault, structured information in Profile and a newly created Journey event; ask “What does my latest report mean?”; show a contextual response; expand “Why am I seeing this?” to show the supporting report/date; ask “What should I do next?” and show a safe response; demonstrate Telugu or Hindi output; and briefly explain the architecture and end-to-end information flow. Implement as one coherent, visually impressive, medically responsible and responsive product. Use clean TypeScript, reusable components, simple data structures, clear naming, minimal dependencies and maintainable CSS. Inspect and preserve existing starter functionality. Keep the interface simple outside and intelligent inside. Build and checkpoint to the Manus Preview; do not publish publicly without a separate request.

Submitting a typed or transcribed question immediately shows the user's message. - [x] **Clear, truthful customer-facing product copy** — Remove every visible use of “Hackathon prototype” and remove the public-facing “Hackathon prototype” label from sign-in, Home, footer, privacy, and document-review screens. ## Latest auth direction — supersedes earlier provider-unspecified default

The newer user instruction explicitly requests email + one-time code, so it supersedes the earlier “Manus OAuth when no provider is specified” acceptance clause. Use Manus OAuth only as a clearly labelled fallback while actual email delivery is unavailable; never label OAuth as email OTP and never simulate a successful delivery.

- [ ] **Email OTP sign-in and account creation** — From the welcome page the user can choose email, enter an address, request a six-digit code delivered to that email, and enter it to create or access their real personal account; real personal accounts have no demo password. Codes are HMAC-hashed at rest, expire in 10 minutes, are single-use, permit no more than five attempts, have a per-email cooldown and hourly limit, and do not reveal whether an address has a registered account. Once verified, return the same secure HTTP-only HTTPS Preview-compatible session and gate the personal workspace on profile onboarding. Keep the Manus login visibly described as a fallback. If Resend lacks an API key/verified sender or delivery fails, show “email sign-in temporarily unavailable,” do not claim an email was sent, and preserve the fallback.
- [ ] **Demo email/password sign-in** — The other welcome choice opens a separate read-only demo login. Display and prefill the sample credentials `demo@healthsayhak.test` / `HealthSahayak-demo`, verify them only against sample constants in the demo UI, and on success show only clearly synthetic data. Demonstrate sample report, prescription, note/discharge document entries with visible document names, categories, dates, and states; do not store the public demo credentials, ask for personal health information, or seed sample records into a real user database. Explicitly say demo credentials are public and only for the synthetic sample.
- [ ] **Opening work-flow board** — The first screen shows, before login, the five clear ordered steps: document upload → inspect/edit AI-found details → user confirms → see confirmed data in Health Vault/Profile/Journey → ask Copilot and inspect source evidence. Keep two separate entry choices: real email-code account or demo login.
- [ ] **Concise hackathon-judge brief** — Provide a short, reachable “For judges” page answering: what everyday fragmented-record problem motivated the product; why continuity and informed user control matter; why this source-linked, human-confirmed timeline is different from a generic health chatbot or file vault; exact frontend, backend, database, AI, storage and email technologies; how and where the prototype is currently hosted/deployed; safety/privacy boundaries; scalability/future potential; and which claims (including ABDM integration and medical diagnosis) are not implemented. Do not describe an un-published Preview as a published production deployment.
- [ ] **Real OTP sender configuration** — Keep Resend key and sender address in protected server-side project secrets, never source or browser assets. The sending address must be verified in Resend. If no authorized key/sender exists, display the honest unavailable state rather than a fake code or successful send. Do not ask the user to paste credentials into chat.


## Latest user-requested refinements — keep open until implemented and verified

- [ ] **Health-only Copilot answers** — Answer health-related questions only. Do not answer general-knowledge, biography or sports questions; specifically, if asked “Who is Virat Kohli?” do not give facts about him. Reply briefly in the selected English, Telugu or Hindi language that HealthSahayak handles health questions, and invite a question about a report, medication, appointment or symptom. Keep relevant health questions working with their safe, source-backed answer. Permit only clearly pronoun-based follow-ups when the current conversation contains a prior health question; do not let an unrelated name or topic pass merely because the chat contains health records.
- [ ] **Health-document relevance check before storage** — For uploads or camera scans, check the actual PDF/JPG/PNG content before any durable project-storage upload or health-document database row. Check image/camera bytes directly; render up to three PDF pages on the server from the original uploaded bytes into in-memory previews (do not trust client-supplied previews) and send those previews for health-only classification. Accept supported prescriptions, lab/diagnostic reports, doctor notes, discharge summaries, medical records, insurance and applicable wellness records only after a positive health-document decision. Reject unrelated, unclear, unreadable or unclassifiable content with a localized explanation; do not persist its bytes or add them to the Health Vault, Profile, Journey or Copilot. Preserve explicit consent and disclose that bytes are sent to the configured AI service before storage.
- [ ] **User-controlled Health Vault removal** — Provide a visible delete/remove action for each user's own uploaded document, with an accessible confirmation. After confirmation, remove the item from the active Vault, delete linked health records and Journey events, remove saved Copilot turns whose citations point to that source, and exclude it from future Copilot context. Never permit a user to remove another person's or synthetic demo record. Explain before the user confirms that the runtime storage service cannot physically erase the uploaded source bytes, so removal hides/de-links the source in HealthSahayak but the bytes remain in managed project storage in this preview; do not claim permanent erasure.
